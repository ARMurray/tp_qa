## The goal, as stated by the project owner (2026-09-24)

- **Stakeholders:** federal agencies and the American public. No deadline;
  sooner is better.
- **Accuracy target:** be **more than 90% sure** that when a location is
  moved, the new location is right. Relocation is meant to be automated, so
  this is **precision of moves**, not recall@k.
- Review is **not** done state by state.

Every modelling decision is judged against that target.

### Round 4 review — out-of-sample precision of "move to #1"

First review of plants the models had never seen (106 decided candidate_pick
plants). **This is the only honest precision read so far.**

| re-ranker score ≥ | plants | right | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.95 | 16 | 93.8% | 72–99% |
| 0.85 | 30 | 83.3% | 66–93% |
| 0.80 | 32 | 81.2% | 65–91% |
| all | 106 | 47.2% | 38–57% |

90% looks reachable at the top of the score range, but n=16 cannot prove it.
`needs_info` fell to 17% (from 32% in round 3) with the population filter.

### Stage 1 owner-keyword finding (stage1_keyword_diagnostic, 2026-10-01)

Reported parcels with a wastewater keyword / utility owner are **99.2%
Correct** in the labels (3 of 330 wrong), but the model — leaning on
`osm_ww` (importance 0.34) — scored such parcels **without** an OSM tag at
mean 0.70 and flagged 25%. In round 4, 6 of the 14 Stage 1 false flags were
this pattern. The two genuine exceptions were large regional utilities
(Renewable Water Resources, Toho Water Authority) that own many facilities.
Response: the OSM rule + `utility_owner` above. A guardrail ("never flag a
utility-owned reported parcel") was deliberately **held** until we see
whether the retrained Stage 1 learns it.

### Name-match features (name_match_benchmark)

Distinctive-token, rarity-weighted owner-vs-facility-name match: 60.8%
recall@1 alone vs Stage 2a 58.5%, 6% damage. Embeddings rescued similar
amounts but broke ~30% of pools — not used. Production module
(`name_match.py`) verified equal to the benchmark method. Name identifies the
OWNER, not the parcel (a city owns many parcels in a pool), so it carries
little weight in Stage 2a and modest weight in the re-ranker; ranks 6th in
Stage 1.

   - `08_diagnose_candidate_coverage` undercounts misses outside the ring

## Operational lessons from this session

- **Work on `master`.** `claude/wip` is retired.
- **No Python on the HPC login node.** Diagnostics ship as a committed
  script + `.slurm` that write to `correction/diagnostics/`, pushed back via
  git (`collect_diagnostics`, `collect_logs`, `preflight_inference`,
  `name_match_benchmark`, `stage1_keyword_diagnostic`).
- **The login shell is tcsh.** No `$(...)`; copy job ids by hand.
  `--dependency=afterok` has failed repeatedly — check `sacct` and submit the
  next step by hand instead.
- **A detector change makes ALL detection output stale** (01b, 01c, 01e) and
  resume cannot tell: re-run with `NORESUME=1`. `check_od_freshness` only
  checks 01b's root; 05b now skips candidate-OD files older than `best.pt`.
- **`04_train_model.py` overwrites the deployed `best.pt` on success.** Back
  it up first; commit only if the new model is clearly better.
- **Labelling is in `detection/OWM_Imagery_Labeler/label_app.R`** (not Label
  Studio). It reads `detection/data/tiles/rgb/png/` at startup; restart it to
  see new tiles. Plant table, "selected as" per tile, label-count chart.
- **Closing a round:** `python -m sync.close_round --round N` from
  `review_app`, then commit `Updates.gpkg`, `app.db` and `data/outgoing/`
  immediately (binary; git cannot merge them). The tile step uses
  `detection/.venv` and only tiles what touches each parcel.
- **Review notes** go in `docs/review_notes/roundN_notes.md`.
