How to read this

Sources. Each plant can have up to five plant-role points from three independent origins: the CWNS record (its point and the geocode of its address), the ICIS facility record (its FRS coordinate and the geocode of its address), and ICIS permit features (influent structures). Points from the same origin are not independent evidence.

Consensus. Plant-role points within 1 km of each other (chained) form a cluster. The cluster with the most independent origins is the consensus. "CWNS point isolated" means the other origins agree with each other and the CWNS point is not in their cluster.

Not scored. External outfalls and industrial users sit away from the plant by design. They are shown for context and measured from the consensus point.

Verified. For plants reviewed in tp_qa, the verified location is shown as a star, and the "CWNS point to verified location" measure is available.
